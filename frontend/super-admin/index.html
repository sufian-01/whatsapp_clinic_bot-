🎙️ Inbound Voice Note Processing (Sarvam AI STT)
Transcribe patient voice notes into clinical booking prompts. When disabled, AI politely asks patient to type.
🗣️ Spoken Voice Note Responses (Sarvam AI TTS)
Reply with natural spoken audio notes on WhatsApp. When disabled, AI sends only text/interactive menus.
📋 WhatsApp Interactive Lists & Quick Buttons
Deliver structured doctor catalogs, slot selectors, and confirmation buttons. When disabled, delivers numbered text.
💳 Online Payment & UPI QR Generation
Generate online payment links and QR codes. When disabled, bookings default to Pay at Clinic (Offline).
❌ Patient Self-Cancellation via WhatsApp
Allow patients to cancel appointments via chat. When disabled, AI directs patient to clinic reception.
🔄 Patient Self-Rescheduling via WhatsApp
Allow patients to change booking time slots. When disabled, AI directs patient to clinic reception.
