5. Test Preparation – Demo Information
Fasting Blood Glucose: The patient may be asked to fast before the sample. Exact preparation should be
confirmed with the clinic before testing.
Lipid Profile: Fasting requirements can depend on the doctor's or laboratory's instructions. Patients
should confirm preparation requirements before the test.
Urine Routine Examination: The clinic may provide a sterile sample container and collection instructions
at the laboratory.
General rule: Test-specific instructions provided by the clinic or doctor should be followed. The chatbot
should not invent preparation instructions when the knowledge base does not contain them.
